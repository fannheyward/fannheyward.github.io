I recently started a **60 Apps Program**: to keep the total number of apps on my iPhone under 60.

I found that about one-third of the apps I installed are not used often. Some apps were downloaded just for the novelty, some offer similar functionality, and some were only used once. They continue to take and waste storage.

New principle: 

- prefer native iOS whenever possible
- prefer web apps for products that require lightweight interactions

I will continue the program until the end of 2026 to determine its feasibility.
